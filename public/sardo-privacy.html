Privacy Policy for SARDO

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application SARDO Attendance Helper for mobile devices.

1. Data Collection & Privacy

This application is designed as an "Offline-First" tool. We do not collect, store, or transmit any student data (including Names, LRNs, or Attendance Records) to any external servers or third parties.

All information entered into the app remains exclusively on your mobile device's local storage.

2. Data Usage

Information entered by the user is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Generating official DepEd SF2 (School Form 2) reports in CSV format.
  • Calculating attendance metrics (ADA, percentages).
  • Automated tracking of Students at Risk of Dropping Out (SARDO).

3. Third-Party Access

We do not share, sell, or trade student information. The app does not utilize third-party analytics or advertising services that have access to your student records.

4. Data Security & Deletion

The security of the student data relies on the security of your mobile device. If you delete the application, all data stored within it will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

5. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
